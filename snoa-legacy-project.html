375 years of continuity

An unbroken story unlike any other in the Americas

From the first Sephardic Jews who arrived seeking refuge and opportunity, across the centuries to the living community of today, Mikvé Israel-Emanuel has carried a story unlike any other in the Americas; a story rooted in courage, built on tradition, and sustained by generations who believed deeply in its future.

Today, that future is in our hands.

This is more than restoration.
More than programming.
More than a celebration.
It is a commitment.
The MIE Legacy Project

A bold vision for the next 375 years

As the MIE congregation celebrates its milestone birthday, JCCPF has identified five interconnected areas of need. Together, they form the Legacy Project, a framework for ensuring that 375 years of history do not become a memory, but remain a living, thriving presence for generations to come.

Preserve the historic synagogue

Protect and restore the communal building that have stood at the heart of Jewish life in Curaçao for nearly three centuries.

Reimagine underutilized spaces

Transform existing assets into sustainable, income-generating properties that fund the community's long-term future.

Support cultural programming

Fund educational, cultural, and community events tied to the 375th anniversary and beyond.

Strengthen global connections

Build lasting bridges between Curaçao and both the worldwide Jewish diaspora descended from this community, and more.

Create pathways for future generations

Build programming and leadership opportunities that give young people a reason to engage with, lead, and belong to this community for centuries to come.

Why now

Moments like this come once in centuries.

This anniversary is not just a time to look back. Rather, it is a rare opportunity to build forward with intention, vision, and collective responsibility.

Without decisive investment today, the physical and communal infrastructure that has sustained the oldest Jewish community in the Americas for centuries is at risk. With it, we can ensure something far more powerful.

The moment
A 375th anniversary is a once-in-a-generation opportunity to galvanize global attention, community pride, and philanthropic support around a shared cause.
The need
Aging infrastructure, limited local resources, and the pressures of sustaining a small community in the modern world require coordinated, strategic investment now.
The opportunity
Descendants and supporters around the world are watching. This is the moment to show them what their connection to Curaçao can build.
The goal
Not a memory.
A living legacy.

375 years of history deserve more than commemoration. They deserve a future as remarkable as the past.

How JCCPF is helping

We identified the need. Now we're raising the resources to meet it.

JCCPF created the Legacy Project framework to bring focus, urgency, and global visibility to the needs of Congregation Mikvé Israel-Emanuel at this extraordinary milestone. We are fundraising now so that meaningful grants can be in place when they are needed most.

We believe that donors around the world: descendants, supporters, and anyone moved by this story, should have a clear, compelling way to contribute. The Legacy Project gives them that.
